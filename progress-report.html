Progress Report 2023–24

HESI Action Group 2023-24 Report: Education for Green Jobs. Report submitted by Debra Rowe and Sarah Hill, US Partnership for Education for Sustainable Development (organizer of the action group). The Education for Green Jobs Action Group continues to be active and productive. Below are deliverables and outputs from the last two years and planned activities going forward. Read the full PDF ↗

Deliverables and outputs

  • 1. Presentations. On behalf of the action group, team members presented at multiple venues, including a THE and UK sponsored European event and on four panels at the CEM Global Clean Energy Action Forum international conference on a variety of education and actions for green jobs topics.
  • 2. Toolkit, Solutions Summits, Global Guidance Document and resource sharing. For the Clean Energy Ministerial, members developed an initiative on workforce development for an inclusive clean energy transition in a green economy. Everyone interested can now access the deliverables:
    • A toolkit of compiled strategies, policies and programs about workforce development for green jobs for a clean energy transition, shared with participants from over 100 countries.
    • Solutions Summits (recordings) focused on workforce development policies and programs for an inclusive, clean energy transition.
    • Key resources compiled and available to all interested stakeholders, including the action group’s co-authored Global Guidance Document on Education for Green Jobs and many other documents on the resources list. The group also helped disseminate others’ work, for example the self-assessment process within the Greening TVET tool created by the International Labour Organization (ILO), which complements its work in multiple countries for green economy workforce development.
  • 3. Accreditation and quality standards. Over the past year, the group co-organized, with two other HESI action groups (Rankings, Ratings, and Assessments; and SDG Publishers Compact), a convening of accreditation and quality assurance agencies to discuss the importance of including education for sustainable development in accreditation, curricula, and quality standards. The Education for Green Jobs action group managed the outreach, invitations and coordination of speakers. In November 2024, it facilitated the event, which included registrants from over sixty countries. Co-organizers included the International Institute for Higher Education in Latin America and the Caribbean (IESALC) and the International Network for Quality Assurance Agencies in Higher Education (INQAAHE). After the event, the group created and shared a compiled resource list with all registrants, titled Accreditation, Quality and Standards Compiled Resources. These efforts are being coordinated with the Greening Education Partnership Higher Education Working Group deliverables.
  • 4. Greening Education Partnership. In 2024 the group became active in the Greening Education Partnership, participating as presenters, facilitators and planners for next steps, and helping create its Higher Education working group. Within the group, it co-designed the deliverables listed below.

Future deliverables (2025–6)

These items are designed to be flexible so they are most useful to participants and recipients working on greening education and education for green jobs in higher education.

  • Continued participation in the Greening Education Partnership Higher Education working group, including planning and facilitating meetings, and possibly the production of a toolkit.
  • Continued follow-up with the accreditation and quality standards for greening higher education participants, coordinating with the other co-organizers above and with the Higher Education working group.

Members and contributors

The action group has connections for over 2000 members and contributors, created originally as a virtual community through the work with YEA. All members of the virtual community are encouraged to join HESI. They can also use the Green Learning Network, which the Green Growth Knowledge Platform contributed to these efforts; hundreds of members are already listed there. Everyone can join at thegreenforum.org, then join the group Green Learning Network to connect with others.

Engagement

Members contribute to the work of the action group in the following ways:

  • Sharing curricula and analyses of green jobs growth and employer needs for workers (like recent IRENA, Power for All, IEA and LinkedIn reports on green jobs and green skills).
  • Sharing resources and strategies for transforming educational and training institutions during Solutions Summits and other convenings.
  • Sharing initiatives (like the Greening Education Partnership and the multi-agency Green Jobs Pact, plus more initiatives).
  • Collaborating on and sharing new jobs platforms (for example, SolarPower Europe created an international platform to match job seekers to employers and to education programs, launched in early 2023).
  • Providing input into content and organization for future convenings.
