HESI Toolkits and Top Action Tips

Learning & teaching

The Higher Education Sustainability Initiative (HESI) is an open partnership between several international agencies and the higher education community to highlight the critical role of higher education in achieving sustainable development. One of its action groups is Education for Green Jobs; see the Progress Reports at the bottom of its page. Another is the SDG Publishers Compact Fellows, with top action tips for researchers, authors, educators, publishers and journal editors. There are more action groups at the main HESI link.
