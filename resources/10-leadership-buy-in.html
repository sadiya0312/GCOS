SDG 4 Scorecard progress report on national benchmarks 2026

Digital Library. The SDG 4 Scorecard provides a country-led framework that enables each nation to set ambitious yet realistic education targets tailored to its own context and track progress toward achieving them. The 2026 Progress Report on National Benchmarks focuses on completion rates, benchmarking progress against nationally defined targets to highlight where progress is on track, where challenges remain, and where further data are needed. The Scorecard supports accountability, informed decision-making, and more effective implementation of the SDGs, turning evidence into action towards more equitable and sustainable education systems.
