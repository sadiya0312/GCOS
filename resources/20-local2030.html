Local 2030: Localizing the SDGs

Networks & communities

Local 2030: Localizing the SDGs is a network and platform that supports the on-the-ground delivery of the SDGs. The network includes local and regional governments and their associations, national governments, businesses, community-based organizations and other local actors, and the international system of agencies. Local 2030 supports local formal and informal leaders in collaboratively incubating and sharing solutions, unlocking bottlenecks and implementing strategies that advance the SDGs at the local level. Via the online platform, participants share tools, experiences, new solutions and guides to support SDG solutions at the local levels. Anyone can create an account and contribute resources.

Toolbox

A core feature: a range of concrete, practical and adaptable mechanisms and instruments that support the development, implementation, monitoring and review of locally-appropriate SDG actions.
