Green Learning Network

Networks & communities

The Green Learning Network is a space where education and training professionals from around the world can find a range of green learning resources and events, share information, and learn from one another. Launched by the GGKP and the Institute for Training and Research with support from the Partnership for Action on Green Economy (PAGE), the GLN aims to inform, inspire, and innovate while facilitating partnerships and collaboration around green learning. It is one of many groups within the green forum platform.

Sign up instructions

Join at thegreenforum.org, then join the group Green Learning Network.
