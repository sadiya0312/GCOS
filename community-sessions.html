Community of Support Sessions: free and virtual

Integrating Sustainability into Higher Education and Society

Would you like to learn from others and gain access to additional resources and strategies? Sign up to be invited to these sessions.

These sessions are brought to you by the Education for Green Jobs and SDG Compact Fellows action groups of the Higher Education Sustainability Initiative in collaboration with University World News. Many sustainability champions are doing great work but are not sharing their successes and how they solved their challenges. This community helps people learn from each other to overcome barriers, increase successes and get support in a timely manner. Shared resources reduce redundant efforts, and increase collaborations across secondary, further, technical and higher education and with other sectors of society (e.g. businesses, multiple areas within governments, NGOs).

Resources include

  • Practical guides and requested materials
  • Action templates and partnership opportunities
  • Compiled strategies and tactics
  • Success stories and trend data
  • And more
